# Town trainers

`trainer-red.glb` and `trainer-green.glb` are web copies of `public/models/trainer/red.glb` and `green.glb`,
which the project owner supplied. Reduced with glTF-Transform, keeping every map: the colour texture at 2048 px and the normal and
metallic-roughness maps at 1024 px, all as WebP; the clips renamed to `idle`, `walk` and `run` (`restpose`
dropped), resampled, and meshes Draco-compressed.
