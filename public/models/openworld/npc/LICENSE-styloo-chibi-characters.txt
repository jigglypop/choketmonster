The trainer-*.glb files are outfits kept from the pack's all-in-one model ("allinonepr.glb", whose
parts share one skeleton; the pack's readme says to delete the objects you don't want):
  youngster  hairone, chemise, ninjasuitshort, shoe, bag
  lass       hairvariant, shirt, skirt, shoe, bag
  backpacker hairvariant.001, chemise, pants, bottes, bag
  picnicker  hairtail, greenoutfit (+belt, necklace), bottesgreen, bag
  camper     hairtail, shirt, ninjasuitshort, bottes, bag
each with character_low, eyes, eyelashes and tooth. Reduced for the web: textures resized to 256 px as
WebP, animations limited to idle, idle-alt (was "iddle.001"), walk, jump and flip, resampled, and
meshes Draco-compressed.
