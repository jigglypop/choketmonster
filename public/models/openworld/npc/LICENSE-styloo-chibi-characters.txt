Files here are the "glb" (metallic-roughness) versions of merchant, student, archer, ninja and knight,
reduced for the web: textures resized to 256 px and stored as WebP, animations limited to
idle, idle-alt (was "iddle.001"), walk, jump and flip, resampled, and meshes Draco-compressed.
